About Asafiya

Property shapes more than a skyline.

It shapes how people begin and end their days, how comfortably they live, and how securely they invest.

Our story

Built around that understanding

Asafiya Holding is a real estate development and investment company entering Ethiopia's evolving property market across residential, commercial and future mixed-use opportunities.

Our developments bring together distinctive design, thoughtful locations, resilient infrastructure and the details that make everyday life work better. The same consideration extends to ownership, through clearer communication, visible progress, professional service and greater accountability from development through handover and beyond.

The measure of a property is not only how it looks when it is completed. It is what life becomes within it.

What guides us

A new benchmark for premium property in Ethiopia

Purpose

To redefine living and property ownership in Ethiopia through exceptional developments shaped by distinction, confidence and enduring value.

Vision

To set a new benchmark for premium real estate in Ethiopia, creating distinctive developments that elevate everyday life and stand the test of time.

Mission

To develop thoughtfully designed residential and commercial properties that combine premium architecture, reliable infrastructure, transparent development and professional service.

Development philosophy

Luxury creates aspiration. Proof creates confidence.

Every major claim we make is supported by a standard, a process or a proof point you can see on the property's own pages.
